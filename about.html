About Manya Tour & Travels

Your Trusted Companion in Devbhoomi Uttarakhand

Dedicated to serving pilgrims and tourists with unmatched safety, authentic local warmth, and reliable transportation across the Himalayas.

Founder & Operational Lead

Mr. Parvinder Singh Sachdeva

Based in the foothills city of Haldwani (the Gateway to Kumaon), Manya Tour & Travels was established with a singular mission: to make pilgrimage journeys across Uttarakhand comfortable, transparent, and spiritually fulfilling.

Whether it is welcoming thousands of devotees arriving at Kathgodam Railway Station for Baba Neeb Karori's blessings at Kainchi Dham, navigating the high mountain passes of Adi Kailash & Om Parvat, or managing complete Char Dham Yatras, Mr. Parvinder Singh personally oversees each guest's safety and comfort.
