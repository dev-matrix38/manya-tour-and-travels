Legal & Data Protection

Privacy Policy & Data Security

Committed to protecting traveler privacy, secure bookings, and transparent handling of pilgrimage data for yatras in Devbhoomi Uttarakhand.

Effective Date: October 2026 • Governed under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, India
01

Introduction & Scope

Welcome to Manya Tour & Travels (hereinafter referred to as "We," "Our," "Us," or "Company"), headquartered near Kathgodam Railway Station / Main Bus Stand, Haldwani, District Nainital, Uttarakhand - 263139, owned and operated by Mr. Parvinder Singh Sachdeva (Mobile: +91 6005957610).

This Privacy Policy describes our practices regarding the collection, storage, processing, and protection of personal data gathered when you visit our website (manya-tour-and-travels), inquire about taxi services, book customized pilgrimage packages (including Kainchi Dham, Nainital Lake, Char Dham Yatra, Adi Kailash, Almora, and Corbett National Park), or communicate with our travel consultants via WhatsApp, phone call, or email.

By engaging with our services or submitting inquiry forms, you expressly consent to the data collection and usage practices detailed in this policy.

02

Information We Collect

To facilitate seamless travel logistics across the mountain circuits of Kumaon and Garhwal, we collect only necessary traveler details:

A. Personal Identification

  • Full Name of Primary Traveler
  • Active WhatsApp & Mobile Calling Number
  • Email Address (for vouchers & itineraries)
  • Home City / State of Origin
  • Number of adult passengers & children

B. Travel & Logistics Details

  • Pickup Date, Time & Location (Kathgodam / Haldwani / Pantnagar)
  • Drop-off Destination & Return Date
  • Choice of Fleet (Dzire, Ertiga, Innova, Tempo Traveller)
  • Special Requirements (Elderly assistance, child seats, luggage volume)

C. Mandatory Government Pilgrimage Documents (Yatra Specific)

For sensitive high-altitude circuits (Char Dham Yatra - Kedarnath/Badrinath and Adi Kailash / Om Parvat), local authorities mandate traveler registration. If requested by the traveler, we assist in processing:

  • Uttarakhand Tourism Development Board (UTDB) Yatra Registration slips
  • Government Photo ID copies (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, or Passport)
  • Inner-Line Permits (ILP) required by SDM Dharchula / Pithoragarh for border zones
03

How We Use Your Information

We process personal data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

✓
Driver & Vehicle Allotment: Connecting you with your assigned commercial hill driver, sharing cab registration number, and live coordination during train arrivals at Kathgodam or flights at Pantnagar.
✓
Custom Itinerary Generation: Designing day-wise travel plans based on weather, mountain road conditions, and temple darshan timings.
✓
Emergency Assistance & Safety: Coordinating emergency contact numbers with Uttarakhand Traffic Police or Disaster Management (SDRF) in the event of unexpected landslides or road closures.
✓
Billing & Tax Compliance: Generating booking vouchers, receipts, and maintaining records in compliance with Indian commercial transport regulations.
04

Payment Security & Financial Data

Zero Financial Data Stored On Our Servers

Manya Tour & Travels does not capture, store, or process debit card numbers, credit card CVVs, net banking passwords, or UPI MPINs.

  • Advance booking tokens are settled directly via encrypted UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) to official merchant account: Parvinder Singh S/O Ajit Singh.
  • Direct Bank NEFT/IMPS transfers are processed directly through your bank's secure interface.
  • Remaining balance is paid in cash or direct UPI to the assigned driver upon pickup or trip commencement.
05

Disclosure to Third Parties

We never sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data. Your information is shared only on a strict need-to-know basis:

  • 1. Assigned Drivers: Only your primary passenger name, pickup address, and phone number are given to your verified commercial driver for pickup coordination.
  • 2. Hotels & Homestays: In packages where hotel lodging is included (Nainital, Almora, Kausani, Joshimath), guest names and ID details are provided to registered properties as required by local hotel guest registration laws (Form C).
  • 3. Legal Authorities: If subpoenaed or required by Uttarakhand State Police, Transport Department (RTO), or District Magistrate during natural emergencies or border checkpoint inspections.
06

Data Retention & Security Standards

We maintain physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to secure your personal data. Customer inquiries and trip records are retained for a standard statutory period of 12 months for tax, invoicing, and dispute resolution purposes, following which non-essential records are securely deleted.

Communication over WhatsApp is protected by WhatsApp’s end-to-end encryption protocol.

07

Your Rights as a Traveler

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act of India, you hold fundamental rights over your information:

Right to Access

You may request a summary of the personal information stored with us.

Right to Correction

Request updates to misspelled names, incorrect phone numbers, or dates.

Right to Erasure

Request deletion of contact records once your yatra is concluded.
